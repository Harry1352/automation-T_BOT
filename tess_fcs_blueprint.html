1.0 System Directory Map

The Tess Flight Control System is an autonomous AI-powered paper trading companion designed to trade on Delta Exchange India. The system runs locally on macOS, inside a Python virtual environment named tess_env located in the project directory ~/Desktop/T_bot.

2.0 Core Risk & Execution Rules

  • Strict 2% Fractional Risk Sizing: Tess strictly caps risk on any single position to 2% of your running balance (e.g. $3.60 maximum risk on $180, or $10.00 risk on $500). Sizing is calculated backward: Size = Max Risk / Stop Loss Distance.
  • Strict 3.5:1 Reward-to-Risk Target: Tess only targets trades with a minimum of 3.5x return relative to its Stop Loss. If a technical setup cannot support this, she is strictly forbidden from entering. No small profits.
  • ADX Trend Squeeze Filter: Tess calculates trend strength. If ADX is below 20, the market is flat/sideways, and she is forbidden from trading, completely eliminating false whipsaw losses.
  • Session Timing: Entries are restricted only to high-volume London (1:30 PM - 5:30 PM IST) and New York (6:30 PM - 11:30 PM IST) session opens.
